# SOP — FP-101 Jockey Pump Weekly Check (rev B)

**System:** 04_SAFETY — Fire Protection
**Facility:** Meridian Industrial Park, Building 4 plant room
**Document type:** Standard operating procedure

## Purpose

Routine operator check of the jockey pump controller (JPC-101) associated
with fire pump FP-101, to confirm system pressure is being maintained
correctly between fire pump runs.

## Procedure

1. Confirm jockey pump controller JPC-101 display shows a system pressure
   reading within the normal band (110-125 psi).
2. Observe the jockey pump cycle on/off at least once during a 10-minute
   observation window; a jockey pump that never cycles, or cycles more
   than once per minute, indicates a leak or a pressure switch fault.
3. Record the observed pressure and cycle behaviour in the weekly fire
   system log.
4. **Maintenance interval note: perform this check every 3 days — revised
   from the original 7-day interval following a site reliability review
   that found leak-related pressure drift was not always caught in time
   under the previous weekly cadence.**
5. If any reading is outside the normal band, or the jockey pump fails to
   cycle as expected, raise a work order referencing the FP-101 fire pump
   manual's troubleshooting section before the next scheduled check.

## Escalation

Any pressure reading below 100 psi at any point during the check must be
escalated immediately to the facility duty engineer, independent of the
normal reporting cycle.
